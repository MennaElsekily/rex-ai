## General Requirements

- Minimum age 16
- Basic computer skills
- Meeting the entry requirements listed for the chosen course

## Required Documents

- Copy of national ID or passport
- Recent personal photo
- Student ID for the student discount

## Placement Test

Applicants for intermediate or advanced courses without the prerequisite course may take a free online placement test.

## English Language Level

Courses taught in English need intermediate English reading skills; no certificate is required.

## Admission Process

1. Choose a course and intake
2. Complete the registration form
3. Take the placement test if needed
4. Pay the fee or first instalment
5. Receive the welcome email with class access
